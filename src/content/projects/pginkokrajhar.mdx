## Problem

PG in Kokrajhar offers paying-guest accommodation for students and working professionals. The business needed a website where people could browse rooms and where the owner could manage listings and bookings.

## What I built

As a freelance engineer I delivered the platform end to end, from requirements to deployment:

- **Listings managed in Sanity CMS**, with room photos served from Sanity's image CDN.
- **Role-based user authentication** on **Supabase**.
- **Availability and booking requests** on each listing.
- **A Next.js frontend deployed on Vercel.**

## Key decisions

**Content in a CMS, accounts in a database.** Listings live in Sanity, so they can be updated without code changes. Accounts and availability live in Supabase.

**Role-based access.** Accounts have separate roles, so each user only sees and does what their role allows.

**Next.js on Vercel** for the frontend and deployments.

## Results

- The site is live at pginkokrajhar.com.
- Listing management, availability and role-based accounts are running in production.
