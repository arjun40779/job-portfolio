## Problem

Horizon Vision Group is a video editing agency focused on short-form content for TikTok, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts. It needed a landing page whose marketing copy it could change quickly, without waiting on a developer.

## What I built

- **A Sanity content model** for the landing page, so the agency's copy and content are structured and editable in the CMS.
- **A Next.js landing page** with services, portfolio, testimonials and contact sections.
- **Images served from Sanity's image CDN.**
- **Deployment on Vercel**, with the page prerendered for fast loads.

## Key decisions

**Structured content over hard-coded copy.** Modelling the page in Sanity means marketing changes are content edits, not code changes.

**A prerendered page.** Next.js serves the landing page prerendered, so it loads quickly for visitors arriving from social media.

## Results

- The site is live at horizonvisiongroup.in.
- The agency updates its marketing copy in the CMS without code changes.
